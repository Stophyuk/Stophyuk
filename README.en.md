I spent two years planning e-commerce websites and three years planning a document app, where I brought in its first AI features and shipped them as a paid plan. From October 2025 I freelanced, taking client services from planning through development to delivery. Today I work from Jeju, Korea as **solutionzip** (문제해결.zip), taking web and app projects from plan to launch.

## Running

- **[Glow ON](https://glowon.kr)** — write a few lines about a shop's day and get them back as a blog post. Web app and Chrome extension. *(Korean)*

## How I work

- Decide what to build, and what not to, before writing code.
- Payments and sign-in can't be undone, so the server checks them again.
- Before handover a person goes through a fixed checklist, and accounts and code go over in the client's name.
